Common Hazards in Cavite City

🌊 Flooding & Storm Surge

Coastal and low-lying barangays are prone to flooding during heavy rains and storm surges, especially during typhoon season (June–November). Monitor PAGASA advisories and move to higher ground early.

🌀 Typhoons

Cavite City is in the typhoon belt. Secure loose objects, charge devices in advance, and stock up on food, water, and medicine before a storm signal is raised in your area.

🔥 Fire

Densely populated areas are at risk of fire spread. Know your nearest fire exit and keep a fire extinguisher or sand/water ready at home.

🌍 Earthquake

Cavite is near active fault lines. Practice "Duck, Cover, and Hold" and identify safe spots in your home away from heavy furniture and windows.

Preparedness Checklist

🎒 Go Bag Essentials
  • Drinking water (1 gal/person/day) and ready-to-eat food
  • Flashlight, power bank, whistle
  • First aid kit and essential medicines
  • Important documents (IDs, certificates) in a waterproof bag
  • Cash, extra clothes, hygiene kit
  • Battery-powered or hand-crank radio
🏠 Before a Storm
  • Charge phones and power banks fully
  • Store extra water in clean containers
  • Secure roofs, windows, and loose outdoor items
  • Know your barangay's evacuation route and center
📞 Family Emergency Plan
  • Agree on a meeting point if separated
  • Save all hotline numbers in every family member's phone
  • Designate an out-of-town contact for relay messages

About This Site

This is a free, open-source, single-file website built to help Cavite City residents quickly find emergency contacts, evacuation centers, and disaster preparedness information — even on slow connections.

It's intentionally lightweight (one HTML file, no frameworks, <30KB) so it can be hosted for free, downloaded for offline use, or shared via USB/Bluetooth during connectivity outages.

Open source — community resource, not an official LGU channel. Always verify with Cavite City MDRRMO/PIO for official updates.
